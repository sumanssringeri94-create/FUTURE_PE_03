# SEO Content Strategy Project  
## BrightSmile Dental Clinic – Bangalore

This project demonstrates a complete SEO content strategy for a local dental clinic in Bangalore.

##  Business Overview

Business Name: BrightSmile Dental Clinic  
Location: Bangalore, Karnataka,India  
Industry: Dental Healthcare  

##  Primary Keyword

“Best Dental Clinic in Bangalore”

### Search Intent:
- Commercial
- Local intent
- Service-based
- High conversion potential

## Content Cluster Strategy

### Pillar Page:
- Best Dental Clinic in Bangalore – Services, Cost & What to Expect

### Supporting Blogs:
1. Dental Implant Cost in Bangalore
2. Teeth Whitening in Bangalore – Procedure & Price
3. Root Canal Treatment in Bangalore – Pain, Cost & Recovery
4. How to Choose the Right Dentist in Bangalore

##  Internal Linking Structure

- All supporting blogs link back to the pillar page.
- The pillar page links to each supporting blog.

This creates a strong SEO content cluster.
